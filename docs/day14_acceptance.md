# Day 14 Acceptance Criteria

Day 14 must produce a Go/Conditional Go/No-Go decision. The decision must be
based on generated artifacts, not on explanation alone.

## Required Artifacts

- `data/minibench/OC-L0-S01-M1`
- `data/minibench/ST-L3-S01-M1`
- `data/minibench/CT-L2-S01-M2`
- `data/minibench/RT-L4-S01-M1`
- framewise ODI CSV files with ODI, AIS, lambda_min, and condition_number
- toy estimated trajectories
- metrics CSV files
- metric-validity table
- sensitivity tables
- figures generated by script
- `reports/day14_gonogo_report.md`
- `results/day14/tables/day14_gate_table.csv`
- `results/day14/manifests/day14_final_manifest.json`

## Minimum Gates

| Gate | Minimum Requirement | Decision If Failed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four sequences | All four sequences generate, evaluate, and plot. | No-Go until pipeline works. |
| H_tilde validity | No NaN/Inf; symmetric PSD within numerical tolerance. | Fix Jacobian, R, D, or epsilon. |
| Straight tunnel weak alignment | Median reliable axis alignment >= 0.70. | Fix geometry, Jacobian, whitening, or reliability logic. |
| ODI trend | ST and RT ODI are clearly higher than OC. | Diagnose scene geometry and whitening. |
| ODI-drift correlation | Spearman rho(ODI, axis drift rate) >= 0.50 on window samples. | Conditional Go only with clear diagnosis; otherwise No-Go. |
| ODI vs alternatives | ODI beats condition_number or lambda_min on at least one primary drift target. | ODI claim not ready; revise metric or reduce claim. |
| Open control | Low ODI and no stable false weak direction in OC. | Fix OC geometry, thresholds, or reliability checks. |
| Sensitivity | Trend is not caused by one magical D or tau_w setting. | No-Go or freeze as weak evidence only. |

## Decision Labels

### GO

All gates pass or any minor failure is unrelated to the core hypothesis. Day
15-30 may implement weak-subspace update. Benchmark expansion remains
forbidden until the update prototype is tested.

### CONDITIONAL GO

The core evidence is promising, but one or two gates need targeted repair.
Allowed repair time is at most three days. The report must list exact fixes and
the order in which to try them.

### NO-GO

ODI or weak direction does not explain directional drift in the minimum probe.
Stop method development. Return to geometry, Jacobian, whitening, epsilon,
toy_lio, or metric-window design.

## Forbidden Day 14 Behavior

- Do not hide failed sequences.
- Do not manually edit figures.
- Do not tune thresholds on final test output without reporting it.
- Do not claim FAST-LIO2 or real-data validity from the toy probe.
- Do not continue to Day 15 full method work without a gate table.
